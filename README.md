## Files

- [`prices.csv`](prices.csv): every price with app, plan, billing, platforms, processing, the maker's exact words, where on the page, the date read and the source URL. The same file as https://yapapp.cc/data/dictation-app-prices/csv.

## Method and limits

- Prices are what each maker's own page showed on the date in `checked`; taxes, regional prices and discounts are not included.
- "Free" rows are free plans or free apps as their makers describe them; their limits are in the `plan` column and the quote.
- We make [Yap](https://yapapp.cc), a dictation app that runs on the device; its price is in the data like every other app's.
